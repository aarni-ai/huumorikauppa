- **Guest posts** on Finnish lifestyle/gift/humor blogs

### Month 5-6: Authority
- **Media coverage** – pitch to Iltalehti, Iltasanomat lifestyle sections
- **Collaborative gift guides** with other Finnish shops

- **YouTube** – gift unboxing videos with Finnish creators
- Monthly target: 15-20 quality backlinks
